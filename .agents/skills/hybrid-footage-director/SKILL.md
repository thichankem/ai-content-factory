# Hybrid Footage Director Skill — The Golden Media Mix for Historical & Disaster Channels

A common flaw of faceless AI channels is relying 100% on synthetic AI video generation. This quickly causes viewer fatigue, uncanny-valley skepticism, and algorithmic penalization for low-effort synthetic content.

This skill equips AI directors to enforce the **Golden Hybrid Media Ratio**:
- **30% AI Reconstruction**: Kling 1.5, Google Veo, Wan 2.1 (Climactic collisions, night ocean sweeps, atmospheric weather).
- **20% Historical Archival Photos**: Authentic restored 4K public domain photography (Real faces, genuine emotion, Ken Burns 3D parallax depth).
- **15% Dynamic Navigation Maps**: Isometric 3D geographic route overlays, radar sweeps, satellite trajectories.
- **15% Declassified Documents & Newspapers**: Stamped government dossiers, telegrams, New York Times front-page headlines.
- **10% Technical Blueprints & Schematics**: Structural cross-sections, compartment failure diagrams, metallurgical stress simulations.
- **10% Kinetic Motion Graphics**: Elegant typography, timeline progressions, memorial numbers and statistics.

## 2. Ethical Disaster & Safety Directives

When directing disaster documentaries with casualties:
1. **Never generate gore or grotesque violence**: Focus on the environmental scale, structural failure, mechanical collapse, and human courage.
2. **Respect the victims**: Use somber lighting (dark blues, amber emergency strobes, desaturated earth tones) and reverent pacing.
3. **Always anchor theories in verified evidence**: Label speculative conspiracy theories as "bác bỏ bởi ủy ban điều tra" (debunked by commission).
